FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions? We've got answers.

What is SmartHire?

SmartHire is an AI-powered resume analysis platform designed to help users understand their resume, skills and job relevance.

Can I analyze resumes for different careers?

Yes. The platform is designed to support technical and non-technical roles, depending on the job profiles configured in the system.

What does an ATS score mean?

It is an estimate based on the checks and criteria implemented by the application. It does not guarantee that an employer's applicant tracking system will accept your resume.

Does SmartHire guarantee a job?

No. SmartHire provides resume analysis and improvement insights. Hiring decisions depend on employers and many other factors.
